Learning techniques ranked by effectiveness (according to research):

1. High: Distributed practice
2. High: Practice testing
3. Moderate: Elaborative interrogation
4. Moderate: Imagery
5. Moderate: Self explanation
6. Low: Mnemonics
7. Low: Re-reading
8. Low: Interleaved practice
9. Low: Underlining
10. Low: Summarization

(From meta-analysis of 242 studies)
