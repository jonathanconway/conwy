The [Reserve Bank of Australia's October 2026 Financial Stability Review](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/financial-stability-assessment.html) points to incident risks from “complex supply chains and operational dependencies” from entities beyond the regulated financial sector.

> Pull-quote: “Incidents can arise at material service providers and propagate through complex supply chains and operational dependencies, requiring authorities to consider a broader set of entities beyond the regulated financial sector. ”

Is this an opportunity for software engineers to add value by proactively monitoring dependencies?

I think so, and wrote about dependency monitoring here: [Dependency monitoring](/articles/dependency-monitoring)

(No doubt others have said it better than me. For example: [Alex O'Callaghan](https://alexocallaghan.com/hardening-npm-dependency-security))
