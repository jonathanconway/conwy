Second thoughts on AI generated specs... maybe they are not far off from being effective. They key is humans thinking more probabilistically.
Specs don't have to be 100% correct and they don't have to be correct 100% of the time. They just have to be correct enough to improve some business metric(s). Maybe we are entering an era of probabilistic business operation.
In a way, maybe it was already this way. We humans like to think we know the exact answer, but in reality, everything is a bit fuzzy.
