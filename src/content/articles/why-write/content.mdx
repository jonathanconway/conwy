Why do I write [articles](/articles), [micros](/micros), [commentaries](/commentaries), etc.?

Why do anything – the first reason is **for fun**!

I don't have a massive audience, so my writing is mostly for myself, though I hope others will find it useful.

It's definitely helpful for learning and understanding concepts. [Studies on learning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2021.581216/full) show that **Elaborative Interrogation** (generating an explanation of a fact or concept) and **Self-Explanation** (where the student explains how new information is related to already-known information) help moderately with learning. Writing triggers me to perform both kinds of activities.

Additionally I sometimes share pieces of what I've written with others, to get their critical viewpoint. These critiques help me correct errors in my own knowledge or thought process.
